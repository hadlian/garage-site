Not in the email

Differences the diff shows that the email does not mention. They may be part of this update or older drift: the earlier file was itself edited after it was last sent.

Power and keypad

  • The Ignition key became a Power key. All four MoTeC / coil / injector / VSS feeds — Black 6/7, Grey 1/2 and Grey 24/25 — now switch on Power == ON. That fixes confirmed fault 1 (Grey 24/25 live with ignition off). It also closes the cheap way to answer open question 2 in the car README, which relied on that fault. The keypad position of the new key is not decoded; pdm32.json assumes K1, where Ignition was.
  • The compressor output (Grey 34/35) is renamed CMPSR PMP and switches on the Power key, instead of Ext Battery > 12.0 V AND IGNITION. The Compressor power key (K11), which no output ever used, is gone. Whether this output powers the compressor or only signals its relay is still open; this file does not settle it.
  • The alternator output (Grey 7, 10 A) is now enabled, always on. data/pdm32.json records the alternator field as on M150 HB9, so which of the two drives it is a question for the loom.

Fuel pumps

  • The old Fuel Pump 1/2 (Black 2/3) are renamed LIFT PUMP / LIFT PUMP 2.
  • Two previously disabled outputs, Mid power out 9 and 10 (Grey 4/5), are enabled as FUEL PUMP 1 / FUEL PUMP 2 — the 31 rpm / 50 rpm pair in item 3.

Protection settings

  • The inductive-load flag was cleared on both lift pumps and both radiator fans, and set on PCM12Vpwr2. Inrush was cleared on Rad Fan 2 and set on both new fuel pumps. The audit's inductive-load warning now lists seven outputs, up from five.

Alarms and display

  • ALT OVER CHARGING was removed.
  • The battery warning icon's threshold moved from 12.0 V to 11.8 V.
  • Page 2 lost the knock (cylinders 1, 2, 4), ignition timing, fuel time, fuel used and Sensor Failure 6 fields. Page 1 lost ECU GEAR for Calculated Gear.
  • Many channels were renamed — RPM 01 → ENGINE RPM, Water Temp 01 → WATER TEMP, Oil P 01 → OIL PRESS, GPS Speed → SPEED, and more. The underlying channel IDs for RPM and coolant are unchanged (15 and 4241), so the fan and pump automations still read the same CAN stream.
  • The SmartyCam overlay went from 9 to 22 mapped functions. The brake-pressure slot is fed from oil pressure, as before.

Still open from before

  • Confirmed fault 2 is untouched: the headlight outputs (Black 12/23) still never switch on, and the High Beam icon still watches the cabin fan.
  • The shift lights still start at 8200 rpm.

What changed in the repo

  • car.json points the PDM at M3 PDM32 2026-10-22.zconf2. Three new log markers (CMPSR_PMP, LIFT_PUMP_2, surge_tank_switc) join the old eight, which stay so logs from before the update still match.
  • data/pdm32.json re-read for the outputs, feeds and keys above. The schematic shows one red fault instead of two, and the three-alarm caveat.
  • 04-config-audit regenerated from the new file. Its check for an alarm "measuring the wrong quantity" read only the first channel in a condition, and flagged LOW FUEL PRESSURE because that channel is rpm. It now passes an alarm if any of its channels has the right unit. Against the old file it still catches ECU Voltage Low.

There are no bolt holes, and at the low points of the contour only about 4 mm of material sits above the flange's underside. An STL has no tolerances or threads; if it is to be machined, a STEP or the native CAD file is the better thing to ask for.
