1. CRITICAL — PDM feed to MoTeC is inverted on Grey 24/25

The harness doc (p.9, "Power from AIM PDM32") assigns:

PDM pinsCircuit per harness docPDM config outputPDM trigger as configured
Black 1+13Starter solenoidStarter (HighPO1, 20 A)Start key → ✓
Black 6+7MOTEC / COIL / INJ / VSSIgn On 1/Ign On2 (MidPO5/6, 15 A ea)IGNITION ON → ✓
Grey 1+2MOTEC / COIL / INJ / VSSPCM 12VPWR (HBPO1, 35 A)IGNITION ON → ✓
Grey 24+25MOTEC / COIL / INJ / VSSPCM12Vpwr2 (HBPO3, 35 A)IGNITION OFF → ✗ WRONG
Grey 34+35Compressor powerAIR SHIFT COMP (HBPO4, 35 A)Ign ON + V>12 → ✓

PCM12Vpwr2 is configured to switch ON only when IGNITION is OFF — the exact inverse of its two sister feeds. Whatever branch of MOTEC/COIL/INJ/VSS lands on Grey 24/25 is dead while the ignition is on and hot when it's off. This was flagged as "verify intent" in v1; the harness doc now proves it's a bug. Fix in RaceStudio: trigger = IGNITION == ON (match HBPO1).

Which branch is on which feed — not in REV 01, but bounded

All 20 pages of REV 01 were read for this on 12 Aug 2026. There is no splice map, no branch table, nothing that assigns MoTeC / coils / injectors / VSS to a particular feed. p.9 labels all three feeds identically. The Black-connector splice numbers in data/pdm32.json are pairing splices — Black 6 ↔ "splice 7", Black 7 ↔ "splice 6", the two halves of one output — not branch assignments. Re-reading the document will not answer this.

What it is bounded by, using one fact from outside the file — the car runs:

With the ignition ON, Grey 24/25 is OFF. So every load the engine needs to run is fed by Black 6/7 and/or Grey 1/2, and whatever is on Grey 24/25 is either unused or duplicated. Two readings survive that, with very different consequences:

  • (a) Orphan feed. Grey 24/25 feeds a branch nothing depends on. The fault is inert and a 35 A output is wasted.
  • (b) Parallel feed. Grey 24/25 is spliced alongside Grey 1/2 (or Black 6/7) into the same branch. The car runs on the surviving feed, so nothing looks wrong — but that branch carries its whole load on one feed instead of two, with no redundancy, and it sits at 12 V whenever the battery cutoff is on, ignition or not.

The symptoms cannot separate them: in both cases the car simply runs. (b) is the one worth knowing about.

The test that settles it — and why it has to happen first

The branch supplies are individually accessible at the 61-pin milspec bulkhead (p.6), so this is a multimeter job, not a loom trace. There is no ignition key on this car — ignition is keypad button K1, and the PDM is awake whenever the battery cutoff is on (Grey 23). So both states are taken with the cutoff ON: K1 ignition OFF, then K1 ignition ON. Probe each to ground:

PinsBranch
GG, HH, JJ, KK, LL, MMCoil 1–6 12 V
s, t, u, v, w, xInjector 1–6 12 V
ZFront VSS 12 V
c / FFCAN 1 / CAN 2 power
B13, B19, C26M150 battery feeds, at the ECU
  • hot with ignition OFF (K1 off, cutoff on) → that branch is on Grey 24/25
  • dead with ignition ON → on Grey 24/25 only — reading (a)
  • hot in both → parallel-fed — reading (b), the case that matters

Do this before flipping the HBPO3 trigger. Right now the inverted fault is the only thing making the feeds distinguishable: Grey 24/25 is the sole output live with K1 off, so anything hot in that state is unambiguously on it. Correct the trigger first and all three feeds switch together — ignition off, everything dead; ignition on, everything live — and the mapping becomes untestable without tracing the loom. The bug is currently doing the diagnostic work for you, and fixing it destroys the evidence.

2. HIGH — PDM automatic logic depends on ECU CAN data that must be re-verified after the MoTeC swap

PDM CAN1 (Black 30/31) ↔ M150 CAN3 (A28/29) is the ECU↔PDM bus — both documents agree on the physical link. What the archive adds is exactly which channels the automation depends on, read out of the trigger formulas themselves:

OutputPinsTrigger, decoded from the formulaChannelFID
Fuel Pump 1 / 2B2 / B3RPM 01 > 50 rpm OR Fuel Pump keyRPM 0115
Rad Fan 1 / 2B4 / B5latched: ON Water Temp 01 > 90.556 °C, OFF < 85 °C; OR/AND Rad Fan keyWater Temp 014241

Both channels carry ChTipoFeatCont=14 — the CAN1 Stream feature, which is the ECU protocol. They do not come from a PDM input pin or a math channel. So the dependency is not an inference from the layout sheet: the two automations read the ECU stream and nothing else. If the stream does not match what the M150 transmits, fuel pumps do not prime on crank and the fans never auto-trigger, and the keypad is the only path.

The CAN1 Stream feature is present and selected, and defines 73 ECU channels (ECU IGN TIME, ECU THROTTLE, ECU CY1–8 KNK, ECU IN/EX CM P1/P2, ECU V BATT, …).

What the archive will not give up

The protocol's identity and its CAN IDs are not in readable form. The device manifest names can1_stream.c1.xml, but the archive contains only can1_stream.c1.xc1: a 4-byte little-endian plaintext-length header followed by a body padded to the next multiple of 8. It is encrypted, not compressed — no standard container decompresses it, and the block structure gives the mode away:

  • CAN1 and CAN1V2 share exactly 304 identical leading bytes — 38 whole 8-byte blocks, block-aligned, because they start with the same plaintext.
  • The CAN1 body is 63 022 blocks but only 11 640 distinct, one repeating 730 times.

Identical plaintext producing identical ciphertext, in 8-byte units, is ECB with a 64-bit block cipher and a fixed vendor key. The companion can1.kff is obfuscated too (byte range 1–159). So "check for RPM on CAN ID 0x___" cannot be answered from this file — that has to come off the device or out of RaceStudio's protocol list.

What that leaves for the bench

  • RPM 01 is on two display pages, so the RPM half is checkable at the car with no laptop: crank and watch it move.
  • Water Temp 01 is on none of the three pages. The channel the rad fans depend on cannot be seen at the car at all — the fans would simply never come on, with nothing on the dash to say why. tools/aim_audit.py now raises this as its own finding rather than leaving it to prose.
  • Many other ECU channels are displayed (ECU FUEL P, ECU IGN TIME, ECU THROTTLE, ECU CY1/2/4 KNK, ECU IAT, ECU GEAR). If the protocol is wrong they all go dead together, which is a faster tell than watching one field.

The ECU↔PDM bus (the most important one in the car) is the only one running a single terminator. It may work on the bench and fail intermittently hot/loaded. Add a 120 Ω resistor at the M150 end of CAN3.

⚠️ Doc conflict: the old "M150 M3" sheet in the project says M150 CAN1 (D17/18) goes to the PDM. REV 01 reassigns D17/18 to the Racespec wideband and moves the PDM to CAN3. The old sheet is superseded — retire or annotate it.

4. Compressor architecture — resolved, one leftover

Now coherent across all three sources:

  • Power: PDM HBPO4 (Grey 34/35, 35 A) → compressor relay box (AS612-98SN: pins 1–3 12 V, 9 = relay 12 V, 10 = relay signal). PDM trigger (ign ON + battery >12 V) is correct for a supply feed.
  • Control: M150 C1 (OUT HB2) → compressor relay trigger, routed via the vehicle-rear connector pin 2.

Leftover: PDM keypad key 11 is still labeled "Compressor power" and does nothing (the config never references it). Remove/relabel the key to avoid a driver expecting it to work.

5. Findings from v1 that still stand (chassis side)

  1. Headlights can never turn on — HighPO2 (B12+23, 20 A) enabled, trigger hard-coded to constant 0. Nothing else drives it.
  2. High Beam dash icon reads the Cabin Fan channel (1037) instead of Headlights (1048) — copy/paste error.
  3. Undocumented live circuits in the layout sheet: transponder (Grey 6, always-on), plus the three MoTeC feeds and compressor feed above — the sheet shows all the grey half-bridge pins as unused. Update it from the table in §1.
  4. Brake/tail (B8) is always-on — fine for tails, wrong if brake lamps are in that circuit.
  5. CAN2 accessory power dual-feed — B20 (LowPO7) and Grey 17 (+Vb OUT) both power "CAN 2" devices; sheet already flags "CHECK MAY BE WRONG." Pick one.
  6. EPS labels — sheet says "EPS 1–4"; reality is EPS1 = B24+25, EPS2 = B34+35, all four spliced to one pump, identical triggers ✓.
  7. Paralleled pairs (fuel pump B2+B3, fans B4+B5, ign B6+B7) have identical triggers ✓ — deliberate, but each half is separately protected; note it in the doc.
  8. Layout sheet typo: Low Power rating cell shows "10:00 AM" (should be 10 A).

Aux naming — not inconsistent, just undocumented

This was carried as a doc nit ("A15 DIG2 = Aux2 but A16 DIG3 = Aux4 — normalise it"). It is not an inconsistency and normalising it would have destroyed the information. "Aux N" is the pin number on the spare-aux 6-pin connector, not the M150 designation. Every one of them lines up, and pins 1 and 6 are the supply and ground, which is why the numbering starts at 2:

Spare-aux pinSheet labelM150 pinDesignationCalled

Two traps fall out of this, both now recorded on the pins themselves in data/m150.json:

  • The sheet's Dig 4 is DIG3, not DIG4. DIG4 (A17) exists and is unused; connector A's own sheet shows DIG3 = "Aux4" and leaves DIG4 blank. Probing A17 because a wiring list said "Dig 4" would find nothing and prove nothing.
  • B12 AV11 lost its aux number in transcription — the connector B sheet reads "Aux 3/ steering wheel turn knob switch", the data said only "Steering wheel rotary knob". Restored, which is what makes Aux 3 visible and the run 2–5 complete.

Confirmed against REV 01 connector A (p.2), connector B (p.3) and the spare-aux table (p.9). Resolved 12 Aug 2026.
