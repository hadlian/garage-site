2. Issues found

Config bugs (in the PDM32 itself)

  1. Headlights can never turn on. HighPO2 (B12+23) is enabled at 20 A, but its trigger math channel is hard-coded to constant 0. No keypad key, triggered command, or other logic drives it anywhere in the config. Either the trigger formula was wiped, or headlight control was never finished.
  1. PCM12Vpwr2 (HBP3, G24+25, 35 A) turns ON when IGNITION is OFF. Its twin, PCM 12VPWR (HBP1, G1+2), is ON when ignition is ON. If both feed the DME/PCM, the second output is live the entire time the PDM is awake with ignition off. If the intent was a redundant second feed, the trigger is inverted by mistake; if it's a deliberate keep-alive, confirm and document it.
  1. "Compressor Power" keypad key (K11) is orphaned. AIR SHIFT COMP (HBP4, G34+35, 35 A) triggers on battery >12 V AND ignition ON — it never references the K11 key. Either the key or the automatic logic is leftover; as configured, the compressor runs whenever the engine's up, and the keypad button does nothing.
  1. High Beam dash indicator reads the wrong channel. The High_Beam display channel references the Cabin Fan output state (channel 1037) instead of Headlights (1048). Copy/paste error — the icon will light when the cabin fan runs.

Documentation gaps (config has it, spreadsheet doesn't)

  1. Four enabled outputs are missing from the layout spreadsheet:
  2. PCM 12VPWR — HBP1, Grey 1+2, 35 A (sheet shows the pins blank)
  3. PCM12Vpwr2 — HBP3, Grey 24+25, 35 A (sheet lists as unused "Half Bridge Output 3")
  4. AIR SHIFT COMP — HBP4, Grey 34+35, 35 A (sheet blank)
  5. Transponder — LowPO10, Grey 6, 10 A, always-ON (sheet blank)

The grey connector is doing far more than the sheet says — all three used half-bridge pairs carry 35 A circuits.

Duplication / conflict risks

  1. Brake/tail output is always-on. B8 ("brake/tail light → wires near steering column") is constant-ON whenever the PDM is awake. Fine for tail/running lights; wrong if the circuit includes brake lamps (no brake switching exists in the config). Verify what that wire actually feeds.
  1. CAN2 accessories are powered from two places. The sheet feeds "CAN 2, smartycam" from B20 (LowPO7, switched output, always-ON in config) and wires Grey 17 (+Vb OUT) / Grey 18 (GND) to "CAN 2" — already flagged "CHECK MAY BE WRONG" in the sheet. The SmartyCam additionally shows external power on sheet 4/4. Two 12 V sources on the same bus risks backfeeding an off output; pick one supply per device.
  1. EPS labels are self-contradictory. The sheet calls B24/25 "EPS 3 / EPS 4" and B34/35 "EPS 1 / EPS 2"; the config has only EPS1 (on B24+25) and EPS2 (on B34+35). It's really two 20 A outputs, all four pins spliced, feeding one pump. Suggest relabeling: B24+25 = EPS1, B34+35 = EPS2, splice noted once.
  1. Paralleled distinct channels (deliberate, but worth a note in the doc): fuel pump = B2+B3 spliced, rad fan = B4+B5 spliced, ECU ign = B6+B7 spliced, EPS = B24/25+B34/35 spliced. Trigger logic for each pair is identical in the config ✓, so they switch together — but each half is a separately-protected channel; if one trips, its partner carries the full load. Also, both "Rad Fan" channels share one physical fan with the same thresholds — no staging.

Minor

  1. Spreadsheet header typo: Low Power Output rating shows "10:00 AM" (Excel auto-formatted "10 A").
  2. Config short names on the always-on grey outputs ("Caveman Sw", "transponde") would benefit from real circuit descriptions in the sheet.

3. M150 side

The M1 package identifies as GPRP Pro (M150) [01.06.0552, July 2022], VehicleId T1, with a Comment naming the owner and the engine (the exact string is in ecu/resources.json; it is not reproduced here because these pages are public) — its calibration (including ECU pin assignments) is encrypted and can't be read outside MoTeC Tune. (Still true as of 14 Aug 2026, and now confirmed from the other side: the 15.7 MB TuneConfig gives 140 IO resource parameters a real address — <Address Base="0" Offset="0x…"/> — and a decode attempt found nothing at any of them in the readable payloads. The values are in the encrypted section formats/m1pkg_map.json documents. M1 Tune decrypts its own format, so it will display these bindings from the package alone; see 03 for why that matters.) The "M150 M3" sheet currently has empty Function columns for all A/B/C/D connector pins, so there is no ECU-side wiring documentation to cross-check yet. Recommend filling in at least: injectors/coils (S54 = 6 cyl), CAN1 to PDM, sensor inputs in use, and the legacy "Power/Sensor Connector" sheets marked as superseded to avoid confusion.
