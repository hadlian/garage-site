Findings

CRIT · Output 29 "Headlights" can never switch on

Enabled at 20 A but its trigger is the constant 0 (c0;), and nothing else drives it.

→ Bind a trigger, or disable the output so it stops reading as live.

CRIT · Output 31 "PCM12Vpwr2" switches on with ignition OFF

Trigger: IGNITION == OFF. Sister feeds AIR SHIFT COMP, Ign On2, Ign On 1, PCM 12VPWR use IGNITION == ON.

→ Set the trigger to IGNITION == ON unless this really is a key-out circuit.

CRIT · Text message "ECU Voltage Low" measures the wrong quantity

It is driven by ECU IGN TIME, which is in deg, while the name says V/mV. Condition: ECU IGN TIME < 12000 deg.

→ Rebind to a V/mV channel. The threshold will be meaningless against the present source, so the warning is effectively stuck.

CRIT · No shift light ever lights below the rev limit

First LED is at 8200 rpm; the profile puts the limit at 7000 rpm. The whole sequence sits above what the engine turns.

→ Rescale the sequence — a usual spread is 6160–7000 rpm.

WARN · Config has been edited since it was last sent to the device

Last modified 01 Aug 2026, last transmitted 25 Jul 2026. What is in this file is not what is running.

→ Transmit in RaceStudio, or treat the device as the master record.

WARN · 4 output(s) are permanently on

16 Can2 AccPo (10 A), 17 Bk Tail (15 A), 19 transponde (10 A), 25 Caveman Sw (15 A). Each is triggered by the constant 1, so it is live whenever the PDM has battery — ignition off, car parked, trailer.

→ Correct for a true always-on circuit; a parasitic drain otherwise. Decide per circuit and move the rest onto the ignition feed.

WARN · 5 motor/solenoid output(s) not flagged as inductive

7 WiperHS, 8 WipeLS, 15 WiperPark, 18 Cabin Fan, 28 EPS1. Motors, pumps, fans and solenoids kick back when switched off; without the flag the PDM does not clamp the transient, and the spike goes onto a rail shared with the ECU.

→ Tick 'inductive load' on each in RaceStudio.

WARN · Warning icon "High_Beam" may watch the wrong channel

It is driven by Cabin Fan — state selector: Cabin Fan == OFF. Nothing in the source name relates to what the warning is called.

→ Confirm the binding, or rename the warning to match what it watches.

WARN · 4 display field(s) show channels no hardware supplies

Tire Pressure 1, Tire Pressure 2, Tire Pressure 3, Tire Pressure 4 — this configuration declares no expansions, and those channels come from an AiM TPMS receiver. The fields render empty.

→ Fit an AiM TPMS receiver, or put a channel the car actually has in those positions. Empty fields on a driver page are worse than no field: they read as a failed sensor.

WARN · 3 alarmed measurement(s) appear on no display page

ECU V BATT, Oil P 01, Water Temp 01. The warning fires and the driver has no number to report, and no way to see whether it is drifting or recovering.

→ Put each on the page that is up when it would fire — the race page for anything that ends a lap early, the in/out-lap page for the rest.

WARN · Keypad button "Compressor power" does nothing

The button is named, so the driver will expect it to work, but no output, alarm or command references it.

→ Wire it to an output, or clear the label.

INFO · CAN Output2 KeyPad_20230405_130416: 120 Ω termination is off in the config

1000 kbit/s. Correct only if the bus is terminated at both ends by other devices.

→ Confirm against the physical bus — a bus terminated at one end passes on the bench and fails hot.

INFO · SmartyCam overlay: 9 of 31 functions mapped

22 overlay slots have no channel behind them.

→ Map the ones you review video for; leave the rest.
