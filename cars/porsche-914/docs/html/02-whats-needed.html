What is still needed, and what each item settles

Everything on the car's page is read from four files: two MaxxECU tunes, the MXP's configuration and one MaxxECU log. These are the things those files cannot say. Each item names what it settles, so it can be skipped if that question does not matter. Most need MTune or RaceStudio and no car.

#Ask forWhyWhat it settles
1 — the big oneA tune saved from the ECU as it is now: MTune connected to the car, File → Save As, the .MaxxECU-save file.The ECU reports firmware 1.153; the newest committed tune is 1.149 and already 27 settings behind what the car ran in April 2025. See 01-dash-log-2026-10-26.md.Every tune value on the car's page becomes the car's current tune instead of an older one.
2 — on the carCheck Lambda A: sensor plugged in, heater powered, no wiring damage. A short log or a screenshot of the live value at idle afterwards.Above 2,000 rpm on 26 Oct it read 1.8–2.4 (very lean) while the dash's own LambdaCAN read about 0.74 (rich), and closed loop added +5% fuel on 30% of those samples.Whether the sensor failed. Until then, closed-loop fuelling is chasing a bad reading and making a rich engine richer.
3 — mostly doneAiM's technical sheets for the MXP and the EPM08 arrived 27 Oct 2026 and are transcribed into data/aim-mxp-1.3-pinout.json and data/aim-epm08-pinout.json, now on the car's page. Two things remain. (a) A photo of the dash's label, or RaceStudio's device information. (b) With the dash live and the engine off, unplug one sensor at a time and note which channel goes open. Channel Expander, LambdaCAN and K8 sheets are still optional.(a) The sheet is the MXP 1.3's, and the configuration names the dash only "MXP"; an earlier MXP may be wired differently. (b) The configuration ties each channel to a physical input index (Ch01–Ch08 are 0–7), but that index 0 is the sheet's "Analog Input 1" is AiM's numbering read in order, not stated.(a) Whether the MXP pin functions on the page apply at all — they are probable until then. (b) Which pin each sensor is on, directly. The EPM08 does not need (a): the configuration names it "EPM08". Its output-to-pin match is by name and probable; switching one output from RaceStudio and watching its LED settles it.
4 — doneA photo of the ECU's label — no longer needed.MTune's ECU Type list (screenshot 27 Oct 2026) has MaxxECU RACE and MaxxECU GEN2 RACE as separate types, and the ECU identifies itself as RACE v13 in the tune it embedded in its own April 2025 log.GEN1 RACE confirmed: the ECU connector tables on the page are the right ones.
5 — doneMTune's Output Config screen — received 27 Oct 2026.It settled both questions: the tune's AUX n is the manual's GPO n (every row agrees), and AUX 9 = 91 is "Lambda sensor 1 heater" on GP Out 9, the internal wideband's heater on pin D1 — not a stray. GP Out 5 is the fuel pump relay.Now on the car's page: the output joins are confirmed and those values are named.
6 — mostly doneOption labels — received 27 Oct 2026: Engine Type 0 is Piston 4-stroke, Trigger Wheeltype 1 is Missingtooth, and the firing order is stored as cylinder minus one (Cyl 1, 6, 2, 4, 3, 5). Fuel Inj Type is still a number.The tune stores options as numbers.Names on the page; recorded in formats/maxxecu_map.json.
7 — doneThe fuel pressure unit — kPa, from MTune's Fuel pressure sensor 1 screen.The one logged pressure was a constant 8.0, which turned out to be the dash's placeholder (item 10).Pressure in the ECU log reads as kPa — confirmed by MTune's CAN input value 1 screen, which feeds the dash's 8 into "Fuel pressure sensor 1 (kPa)" at a resolution of 1.
8 — a glanceDoes the rev counter on the dash work?Every dash alarm is gated on the dash's own RPM input (37-pin, pin 15), and that channel is not in the 26 Oct log; engine rpm there comes from the ECU over CAN. See 03-tune-and-alarms.md.Whether the dash's alarms can fire at all. If the rev counter is dead, pin 15 is not wired and the alarms need re-pointing at the ECU's rpm.
9 — on the carBattery voltage at idle, warm, with the fans and pumps running.The 26 Oct session idled near 1,350 rpm for about 10 minutes at 11.3–11.8 V (dash and ECU agree) before driving at about 13.3 V, which would have held the Batt_Fan alarm on throughout.Whether the charging system keeps up at idle.
10 — the dash, importantPoint the dash's CAN Output1 at real channels, or stop the ECU using them: frame 0x1 bits 0–15 ("Fuel Pressure" on the ECU) and bits 16–31 ("Left Lambda", lambda bank B).Both are fixed placeholder values in the dash configuration, 8 kPa and 0. The ECU tracks injector flow against that 8 kPa, and bank B (cylinders 1–3) has no lambda at all. The dash measures the real fuel pressure and lambda and sends neither. See 03-tune-and-alarms.md.Whether fuelling compensates for the real pressure, and whether bank B can ever be corrected.
11 — on the carWhich Bosch sensor is fitted for the ECU's internal wideband: LSU 4.2 or LSU 4.9.The tune is set to LSU 4.2, and MTune warns a wrong setting can damage the sensor.Whether the setting explains Lambda A's lean reading.
12 — doneMTune with the 1.153 tune loaded — received 27 Oct 2026: closed loop is "Wideband (target=lambda table)" with ±5% limits; the timing pulls are the acceleration correction; fuel adaptation and shift cut are off; the ECU Type list settles GEN1 (item 4).
13 — for whoever tunes the carA look at the base fuel table.In April 2025, with the ECU's sensor still reading, Lambda A ran about 0.69 against a target of 0.87 above 2,000 rpm. Closed loop is limited to ±5% and only acts above 90 °C coolant, so it cannot close a gap of about 20%. The dash's LambdaCAN reads about 0.74. See 03-tune-and-alarms.md.Whether the engine is meant to run that rich, and part of the 88% injector duty.
14 — on the carCheck three dash sensors: oil filter temperature, RR_Inside, Track_Temp.The filter read 21–29 °C all session while the oil tank ran 67–89 °C; RR_Inside sat flat at −13.2; Track_Temp read −24 to −3 °C with intake air at 27–35 °C.Whether those channels measure anything. Every oil-filter alarm depends on the first.
15 — a settingWhat the ECU's coolant warning should be.It is 187.8 °C, which is 370 °F exactly — a Fahrenheit number in a Celsius field, by the look of it. As set it can never fire.Whether the ECU warns about coolant at all.

When an item lands, the file goes into source/ or logs/ with garage.py add-source / add-log, and the status it settles moves in the same commit.
