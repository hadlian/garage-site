Z4 tuning history — what the twelve tunes actually changed

On the count. source/m800-tunes/ holds 18 .e35 files as of
11 Aug 2026, and the number keeps moving. Twelve are calibrations and are
what this doc analyses — the ten in the lineage below plus the two foreign
files. The other six (DigTest, SensorTest, IgnComps, save4,
AuxTest, AxisAttrib) are controlled edits for the IO and format map, not
tuning: Fuel Main and Ign Main are byte-identical to the current tune in all
six, checked, so nothing in this doc moves when another one arrives.
LambdaChange is a controlled edit too, but it predates this doc and sits in
the lineage diagram, so it is counted among the twelve.

Written 2026-08-07, the day the fuel and ignition tables became readable (formats/e35_map.json, calibration_tables). Everything below comes from parsers.motec_e35.read_tables over source/m800-tunes/; reproduce any diff with tools/diff_e35.py, which decodes table runs to cells. File timestamps are useless (everything was copied into the repo on one day), so ordering rests on names and whole-file byte distance — stated where it matters.

The one-line history

The tuning has been frozen since 2_1_25. Fuel and ignition in the current tune (12_19_25test) are byte-identical to Z4 config 2_1_25.e35; every save since — 2_1_25test, LambdaChange, 12_19_25test — changed IO, logging and sensor setup, never a table cell. Ignition has been touched once in the car's recorded life.

The Stroker404 discrepancy — worth a decision

Z4-3.4Stroker404whp is not a different calibration: it is 2_1_25 with six fuel cells raised in the low-load 1500–2500 RPM region, and its centerpiece is (Effcy 5, RPM 2000): 12.5 → 16.2 %IJPU (+3.7).

That cell is a pothole in the current tune: its neighbours read 16.9 (at 1500 RPM) and 13.5 (at 2500 RPM), and 12.5 sits below both — the current table's sharpest low-load kink (2nd difference +5.4). The Stroker edit fills exactly that hole.

Answered 2026-08-07: the car has the 3.4 stroker. So the current tune really was running pre-stroker fuel in that region — the stroker branch never made it back into the 2_1_25-descended line. The stroker branch changed three calibration regions in total, not one — but those three are two things, not three:

  • Group 1: the six Fuel Main cells.
  • Group 2: a table and its own axis, which is one edit stored in two places. The u8 curve at 0x057B4 (head 6.0 → 25.0, decaying with RPM) is the table; the run at 0x0BD8E is its RPM axis, and the stroker slid it from 900–1800 (10 sites) down to 500–1500 (11 sites). They were originally written up here as independent candidates with separate guesses — "cranking/initial enrichment" for the one and "idle-region RPM sites" for the other. They are not independent: the exe schema types 0x0BD8E as a 20×1 axis, its used-site count tracks the table's used-cell count on both the current line (10 and 10) and the stroker branch (11 and 11), and the map now records it at status probable as that table's axis. Because they pair, they can only move together — stroker values on current breakpoints would be a mismatch.

What Group 2 is not: it is not accel enrichment. That name was a guess from the table's shape plus the Fuel > Accel Enrichment menu, and it was retracted on 9 Aug 2026 (d91a3f8) after all seven Fuel Accel/Decel screens were opened and checked — Accel Clamp, Accel Clamp Comp, Accel Decay, Accel Sens and the three Decel tables — and none matches this table's values or its 500–1500 stroker axis. It was the second wrong name for the region (the first was "cranking curve"). It stands as unidentified; the screens not yet checked are Fuel > Overall Trim, Second Load Table, Secondary Injection and Bank Trims.

The fix that was built: Z4\Z4 config 8_7_26 stroker merge.e35 — the current tune with all three stroker regions copied in verbatim, on top of the current IO and logging, built after verifying the current tune still matched 2_1_25 byte-for-byte in every merge region. All three regions went in together deliberately: merging only the fuel cells would, on the reasoning of the time, have produced a fuel/cranking combination no engine ever ran.

Reviewed 9 Aug 2026 in ECU Manager, and the plan was reversed (d91a3f8). The review split the merge in two and shipped only half:

  • Group 1 — the six off-idle Fuel Main cells. Verified byte-exact in ECU Manager. A clean fix, and the one that answers the pothole above.
  • Group 2 — the 0x057B4 table and its 0x0BD8E axis. Held back. The screens the "accel enrichment" guess predicted were opened and none of them matched, so the table is unidentified — and an unidentified table is not something to send to an engine, however good the values look. See the Stroker404 section above.

So the shipped artefact became Z4 config 8_9_26 offidle only.e35: Group 1 alone, none of Group 2. The reasoning that "merging only the fuel cells would produce a combination no engine ever ran" did not survive the review — it rested on Group 2 being cranking enrichment, which it is not known to be.

Current-table quality notes

  • Fuel, 4000–4500 RPM, load 70–100: an abrupt +7 to +8 ridge at 4500 RPM followed by a drop (2nd diff −10 at load 90). This region is exactly what the QRW23 → 2_1_25 dyno pass shaped, so it is probably deliberate (S54 airflow dip), but it is the roughest part of the table.
  • Fuel, mild non-monotonicity: at 1500 RPM fuel falls as Effcy rises, 50→60→70 reads 42.0 → 41.4 → 40.6; same at 2500 RPM for 90→100 (49.0 → 48.8). Under 1.5 %IJPU each — noted, not alarming.
  • Ignition shape: the 11→15 step off the 0-RPM column is cranking vs running timing, and the 12→19→31 sweep at 2000–3000 RPM low load is the idle-to-midrange transition — normal M800 furniture, listed here so nobody "smooths" it by accident.
  • Ignition, load 110–200: flat 20/22° defaults, never tuned. The engine never sees those loads naturally; only relevant if boost ever happens.

What this sets up

The first suggestion of the suggestions layer was delivered and then reviewed and cut down — which is the layer working, not failing. The stroker merge went into ECU Manager on 9 Aug 2026 as three regions and came out as one: Group 1 shipped, Group 2 held back because its table has no name. The next step is not a bigger merge but a screenshot — Fuel > Overall Trim, Second Load Table, Secondary Injection or Bank Trims, one of which should be the 0x057B4 table. Also outstanding: rebuilding the off-idle file, which was not retained (see above), and log-driven lambda-vs-aim corrections now that tools/fuel_correction.py and garage.py compare exist.
