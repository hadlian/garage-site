Load axis assumption: Effcy % = Throttle Position. Supported by the dash log — Manifold Pres sits at ~100 kPa even at 8–30% TP, which is a baro reading on an ITB engine; the fuel load is TP.

Finding 1 — the WOT lean of 2024 has largely closed

Oct 2024: +5 to +8% lean at wide-open throttle everywhere from 4000–6500 RPM, with the ±5% short-term trim railed. Feb 2026, same table cells: within ±3% (+3.1 at 5500, −2.8 at 8000). Different season (winter air, and Fuel BAP/AT comps active), different fuel, possibly injector service in between — the logs do not say why, only that at WOT the current table now nearly matches its aim. Nothing here is worth a table edit on its own.

In 2024 the banks tracked each other ~2% apart. In 2026 they are 6–10% apart in this region — and only in this region; at WOT the banks still agree. A lean that grew over 16 months, is worst at small pulse widths, differs by bank, and vanishes at WOT reads as mechanical, not calibration: ITB sync/balance drift, a vacuum leak on one bank's runners, or a lagging injector. Do not fix this in the fuel table — a +15% cell edit would feed the healthy bank 8% rich to mask the sick one.

La1 reads the front bank, cylinders 1–3. Established 2026-08-09 by Harry with a coil-pull test at idle — no document records it; the .e35 carries no pinout, the PDM only powers a single "lambda heater", and no Z4 harness scan exists (the M3 has 20 pages of them; the Z4 has none). This is the repo's only wiring fact for the lambda sensors, and it came from the engine, not a drawing.

So the lean bank is the FRONT one: inspection order is front-bank ITB sync/balance, vacuum leaks on runners 1–3, then injectors 1–3. One wrinkle worth carrying into the garage: the TC1–TC6 channels are confirmed as EGT 1–6 on the E888's pins A9–A14 (Input Pins Setup, 2026-08-09), so the channel chain is solid — but which physical cylinder each probe sits in is still only implied by the numbering, and the front bank's EGTs average COOLER than the rear's, where a lean bank should tend hotter. Either the probe-to-cylinder routing, probe depths, or the simple story is wrong; a post-fix log will say which.

Finding 3 — the EGT cross-check is inconclusive but interesting

The dash log's TC1–TC6 show a large, load-stable spread: TC2 coldest, TC1 and TC4 hottest, ~150–180 °F apart at both part throttle and WOT. That is probe placement as much as mixture, and it neither confirms nor refutes the bank asymmetry. A same-probe before/after comparison (after an ITB balance, say) would be meaningful where absolute values are not.

Status

No suggested tune file was produced from this analysis, deliberately. The 2024-era findings that motivated one are superseded by finding 1, and finding 2 is a garage job first. The stroker merge (02-tuning-history.md) is unaffected by these findings; it has since had its ECU Manager review (9 Aug 2026) and was cut to Group 1 — the six off-idle Fuel Main cells — because Group 2's table could not be named.

Closed loop was active only ~9% of the Feb 2026 session, and La1 Long Term Trim is flat zero in every log — the corrections above are mostly raw lambda error, not trim-laundered.
